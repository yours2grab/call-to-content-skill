# Call Intake

Two versions: the first-run version (asked once, during Step 1's voice setup, saved as defaults) and the every-run version (asked after the transcript is in hand, one question plus a one-line confirm).

---

## First-run version (asked once, folded into voice setup)

Asked alongside voice setup, becomes `voice-template.md` Section 11.

### Anonymize real names and companies by default?

Recommended: yes. Feeds the anonymization rule in `SKILL.md`'s hard rules: no real client or company name appears in a draft unless explicitly confirmed OK for that specific run.

### Anyone or any company that's always off-limits, no matter what's said on a call?

A running list, not a one-time answer, add to it any time.

### Default number of drafts per run?

Usual default: 3.

---

## Every-run version (after Step 1 is already done)

### 1. Whose call is this, and what's your role in it?

Always asked fresh, this genuinely changes call to call. Client call, sales call, internal team call, or a podcast-style conversation. Determines who "I" is in the drafts and which content types make sense. A client call points toward lesson/case-study angles. A sales call points toward objection-handling and ICP-insight angles. An internal call points toward building-in-public angles.

### 2. One-line confirm of the saved defaults

> "Same as usual — anonymize names, {standing off-limits list}, {default_draft_count} drafts — or anything different for this one?"

Only dig into a full follow-up question if they flag something different. Otherwise, proceed straight to mining.

---

## Before either version

If the pasted transcript is under roughly 500 words, warn the user there may not be enough raw material for a full batch of distinct angles, and ask if they want to proceed anyway or add more transcript.
