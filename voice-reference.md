# Fallback Voice Reference

> This file is loaded when `voice-template.md` has `filled_in: false`.
> It's a working fallback, not a substitute for doing your own voice setup in Step 1. When it's active, every draft gets `[VOICE NEEDS CUSTOMIZING]` flags to remind you the drafts sound like this reference, not like you.

---

## Core identity

A builder who writes like he talks: plain words, first person, no jargon. Direct without being cold. Comfortable admitting what didn't work, not just what did.

**Voice calibration:** a WhatsApp message to a friend, not a LinkedIn "thought leader" post.

---

## What shapes the voice

- Opens with a scene or a line of dialogue, not a topic sentence
- One idea per post, developed fully rather than three ideas skimmed
- Story, then the insight, then the concrete takeaway, in that order
- Talks to the reader directly, "you" not "people" or "founders in general"
- Comfortable being specific: real numbers, real moments, not generalities

---

## Sentence rhythm

### When it's working
- Clear, no unnecessary words
- Sentence lengths vary, short and medium and one longer carrying sentence, then a snap back
- Reads like it was said out loud, not typed for an audience

### Delete triggers (when it's not working)
- Vague hooks that could open any post on any topic
- The same sentence structure repeated down the page
- Stacked negatives ("No X. No Y. No Z.")
- Horizontal dashes or em dashes used as punctuation

---

## Formatting rules

- Hook: one line, max 8 words, no closing period
- 1-2 sentences per paragraph, heavy white space
- 150-300 words preferred, 400 hard max
- Link (if any) in the first comment, not the post
- No bold, no ALL CAPS, no more than one emoji if any
- Ends on a real question or a one-line takeaway, never a hard CTA

---

## Banned words and patterns

actually, boundless, impeccable, leverage, synergy, unlock, empower, revolutionary, game-changing, best-in-class, landscape, dive in, deep dive, robust, seamless, holistic, paradigm, optimize, scalable, innovative, navigate, elevate, utilize, streamline, cutting-edge

"In today's fast-paced world," "Here's the thing," "Let's dive in," "What if I told you"

---

## The test

Read it aloud. If it sounds like something a real person would actually say to another real person, it's right. If it sounds like it was written to perform expertise, rewrite it.
