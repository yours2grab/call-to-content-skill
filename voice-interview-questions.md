# Voice Interview — 50 Questions

Used only if you pick the guided-interview path in Step 1 of the skill (instead of pasting an existing voice doc). Ask these one at a time, or in small clusters if the user prefers to move fast, waiting for real answers, not skipping ahead. Assemble the answers into `voice-template.md` at the end and show the result before flipping `filled_in: true`.

Don't ask all 50 if the user is clearly in a hurry, front-load the ones marked **(core)** and let the rest fall away if time is short, note in the summary which sections were skipped. The `(core)` label is for you only, never say the word "core" to the user, if they're in a hurry just say "let's start with the most important ones."

---

## Identity & positioning (core: 1-3)

1. **(core)** What do you actually do, in one sentence, for who?
2. **(core)** What's your background that's relevant to why people should listen to you on this?
3. **(core)** What's the "why" behind your work, the thing that made you start doing this?
4. Where are you based, and does that show up in how you write or what you reference?
5. What's a detail about you that isn't obviously relevant to your work, but that you'd still want in a post if it fit?
6. If someone described your energy on a call in one word, what would it be?

## Core beliefs & opinions (core: 7-8)

7. **(core)** What's one thing you believe about your industry that most people in your audience don't yet?
8. **(core)** What's a common piece of advice in your field that you think is actually wrong?
9. What's something you used to believe and changed your mind on?
10. What's a topic you could rant about for 10 minutes without notes?
11. What's the most contrarian thing you've said out loud to a client or colleague recently?
12. What do you wish people asked you more often?

## Sentence rhythm & word choice (core: 13-15)

13. **(core)** Do you write in short punchy sentences, longer flowing ones, or a mix?
14. **(core)** Do you use contractions (don't, can't, it's) or write things out fully?
15. **(core)** Give an example of a sentence you'd actually write versus one that sounds nothing like you.
16. Do you swear, ever, in professional writing? If so, how much?
17. Do you use numbers as digits (3) or words (three)?
18. Do your sentences tend to start with "I" a lot, or do you avoid that?
19. When you're excited about something, how does that show up in your writing? Exclamation points, short sentences, something else?
20. Do you use analogies or metaphors often, or do you write more literally?
21. Is there a rhythm or cadence you've noticed in your own writing that you like?
22. Do you like starting sentences with "and" or "but"?

## Banned words / phrases you personally avoid

23. **(core)** Is there a word or phrase that makes you cringe when you see it in someone else's LinkedIn post?
24. Any corporate jargon you specifically refuse to use?
25. Any phrase you've used before that you now regret and want to retire?
26. Do you avoid buzzwords in your industry specifically, even ones others use constantly?

## Hook style preference (core: 27-28)

27. **(core)** When you scroll LinkedIn, what kind of opening line actually stops you? A claim, a question, a scene, a number?
28. **(core)** Would you rather open with something blunt and confident, or something curious and understated?
29. Do you like starting with a story ("Last week, a client told me...") or a direct statement ("Most founders get this wrong")?
30. Is there a hook style you've seen and hated, that you want to avoid?

## Closer / PS style (core: 31-32)

31. **(core)** How do you usually end a post? A question to the reader, a takeaway line, a call to action, or you just... stop?
32. **(core)** Do you like a PS line, or does that feel gimmicky to you?
33. If you ask a question at the end, do you want real engagement or is it more rhetorical?

## How you refer to clients/team in stories (core: 34)

34. **(core)** When you tell a story about a client or colleague, do you use their real first name with their role, or keep it fully anonymous ("a client of mine")?
35. Is there anyone (a specific client, a competitor, a company) you should never name even indirectly?
36. Do you like giving people in your stories a nickname or description instead of a name ("a founder I worked with", "someone on my team")?

## Off-limits topics (core: 37)

37. **(core)** Is there anything you'd never post about, even if it made a great story?
38. Any topics that are technically allowed but you'd rather stay away from (politics, religion, specific competitors)?
39. Are there numbers you'd never share publicly (revenue, client count, pricing) even if they're true and impressive?

## Format habits

40. Do you like short one-line paragraphs with lots of white space, or denser blocks of text?
41. Do you use emojis at all? If so, how many is too many?
42. Do you ever use bullet points or arrows (→) in posts, or do you prefer straight prose?
43. Do you put links in the post itself or in the first comment?
44. Do you use hashtags? If so, how many, and which ones do you actually use?
45. How long is too long for a post, in your opinion, regardless of any rule this skill has?

## Wrap-up (core: 46-47)

46. **(core)** Is there a post you've written before that you're genuinely proud of? Paste it if you have it, this becomes one of your 3 reference posts.
47. **(core)** Is there a post you've seen from someone else that felt exactly like the tone you're going for?
48. What's one thing about your writing that you know is a weakness, that you'd want the drafts to avoid?
49. What's one thing about your writing that's a strength, that you'd want the drafts to lean into?
50. Anything else about how you write or sound that hasn't come up yet?
